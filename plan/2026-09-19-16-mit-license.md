`Copyright (c) 2026 Olaf Krasicki-Freund`. Preserve the template wording.
The repository is already public. Keep application files, `flake.nix`, and
`manifest.json` unchanged. A README statement alone is insufficient, and
other licenses or custom terms do not meet the owner's MIT request.

validation. After merging, use a follow-up branch and reviewed revert or
